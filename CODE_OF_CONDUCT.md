# Code of conduct

Keep issues, pull requests and reviews focused, respectful and useful. Critique behavior and technical choices without personal attacks. Respect other people's privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, spam, deliberate disruption and publishing someone else's private information are unacceptable.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, close threads, restrict participation or block accounts according to the circumstances. Good-faith disagreements and reports are welcome.

For a moderation concern, open a support issue requesting a private moderation contact without posting names, personal information or incident details. Share details only after a private channel has been agreed. Security reports belong in the channel described in SECURITY.md.
